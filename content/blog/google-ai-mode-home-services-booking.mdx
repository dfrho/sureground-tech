When a homeowner asks Google for a plumber who's available today, the answer may now include a booking link. On November 4, Google added booking and quote links for U.S. home services to AI Mode. The links are pulled from partner platforms Angi, Jobber, Housecall Pro and Thumbtack, with ServiceTitan coming soon ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-mode-home-services-booking-quote-links/595871/); [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-home-services-booking-42459.html)). No mention from Google yet on how business rankings are determined ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-mode-home-services-booking-quote-links/595871/)).

Not all the booking partners are live: [Housecall Pro's press release](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/housecall-pro-google-ai-mode-integration/) says "coming soon", while Thumbtack's plan is spotty on details. As for the booking commission, Jobber and Housecall Pro state no lead fees for these requests.

## What changes operationally

Bookings coming from Google land in the partner platforms as usual. The work is mostly in your settings and your process. Before the requests arrive:

1. **Double check your business hours and availability.** Out-of-sync calendars can frustrate the booking process and lose customers.
2. **Align on who confirms bookings.** Jobber's settings for this are optional and default to them confirming assessment bookings automatically unless vendor approval is toggled on ([Jobber Help](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-and-google-ai-mode-integration/)).
3. **Align and train internally on who owns your response.** Homeowners asking for same-day help expect a same-day answer.
4. **Check that your Google Business Profile matches your listings.** Jobber requires your Jobber account address match your Google Business Profile's ([Jobber Help](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/jobber-and-google-ai-mode-integration/)). Make sure your hours, service area and address are consistent across the board.
