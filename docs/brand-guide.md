## 1. Brand Summary

SureGround is an AI engineering and enablement firm for CEOs and owners of small and mid-size companies. Most leaders want AI working in their business but don't want to be the guinea pig. We've walked this path before, so we take clients across on proven ground: we design the systems, build them into real workflows, and train teams until they own them.

The brand is technical, precise, and proof-driven. It should read like a seasoned builder who ships, and earn trust from both the CEO who is nervous about AI and the technical lead who wants to see the architecture.

**Core services**

- AI engineering: agents, integrations, data pipelines, and evaluations in production
- AI enablement: training, playbooks, and adoption programs for teams
- AI business transformation: use-case strategy, roadmaps, and operating models

## 2. Name and Story

**Name:** SureGround Tech (changed from SureGround by David, 2026-11-14; the domain stays sureground.tech)

**Primary story:** You don't have to be the AI pioneer. We've walked this path, and we'll get you across on sure ground.

**Second story (for technical buyers):** In AI, "grounding" means anchoring a model's answers in verified data. SureGround builds AI that stands on facts, not guesses.

**How to write the name**

- In the logo, titles and formal spots (copyright, legal pages, boilerplate, email footers, first mention on a page): **SureGround Tech** ("SureGround" one word, capital S and G, then "Tech").
- In running copy after the first mention, the short name **SureGround** is fine (for example "Why SureGround").
- In plain text where styling is unavailable: SureGround Tech.
- Never: Sure Ground, Sure-Ground, Shureground, or SG on its own.

### The mark

Three solid steps rising inside a rounded square tile, with a Volt node resting on the top step. It shows ground that has already been cut and proven: the client steps where we have already stood. The steps also read as an ascending chart, which ties the mark to measurable results.

### The wordmark

**Sure**Ground Tech

- "Sure" in Bone (#F2F2EF) on dark backgrounds, or Carbon (#0E1116) on light ones
- "Ground" in Signal Blue (#4C7DFF) on both
- " Tech" in the same color as "Sure", same size and weight, after a space
- Set in Space Grotesk Bold 700 with -2% letter spacing

### Versions

| Version          | Tile        | Steps  | Node | Use                                 |
| ---------------- | ----------- | ------ | ---- | ----------------------------------- |
| Primary (dark)   | Signal Blue | Carbon | Volt | Website, decks, social              |
| Reversed (light) | Carbon      | Bone   | Volt | Light backgrounds, print, documents |
| Mark only        | Either      |        |      | Favicon, avatar, app icon           |

### Rules

- Clear space around the logo equals at least half the tile's width.
- Minimum size is a 24 px tile on screen, or 0.25 in in print.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or add effects.
- Do not recolor the node anything other than Volt.
- Do not split the wordmark across two lines.

**Signature details**

## 7. Brand Voice

### Personality

| Trait              | What it means                           | In practice                                                                            |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Seasoned           | We have done this before and say so     | "We've shipped this pattern for teams like yours," not "we'll explore what's possible" |
| Precise            | Specific verbs and real numbers         | "Cut ticket triage from 4 hours to 20 minutes," not "streamlined support"              |
| Proof over promise | Show the work                           | Architecture diagrams, demos, before and after results                                 |
| Steady             | Lower the temperature; no hype, no fear | Name the risks, then show how we handle them                                           |

### Tagline

**AI on solid ground.**

Alternates:

- You don't have to be the pioneer.
- We've walked this path. We'll get you across.
- Grounded AI, engineered into your business.

### Boilerplate

**Short (one line):**
SureGround Tech is an AI engineering and enablement firm that puts AI to work on proven ground.

**Standard (about 45 words):**
SureGround Tech helps business leaders adopt AI without becoming the experiment. We design, build, and deploy AI systems grounded in your real data and workflows, then train your teams to own them. Our work spans agents, integrations, evaluations, and adoption programs, all built on patterns we've already proven.

### Do

- Lead with proof: past results, proven patterns, and real numbers.
- Reassure with specifics, not adjectives.
- Translate any technical detail for executives in one line.
- Say what AI should not do in a given workflow.
- Use "grounded" deliberately; it carries both stories.

### Avoid

- Pioneer language: cutting-edge, bleeding-edge, first-of-its-kind, uncharted, frontier
- Hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, magic, unleash
- Fear-based urgency ("don't get left behind")
- Walls of text
- Emojis and em dashes in brand copy

### Before and after

| Instead of                                      | Write                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| We help you pioneer cutting-edge AI.            | We put AI to work using patterns we've already proven.         |
| Don't get left behind in the AI revolution.     | Adopt AI at a pace your team can trust.                        |
| Our holistic approach unlocks enterprise value. | We start with one workflow, measure it, then scale what works. |
| AI will transform your customer experience.     | Your support team answers routine tickets in half the time.    |

## 8. Layout and UI

- **Corner radius:** 10 px for cards and images, 8 px for buttons, 12 px for the logo tile
- **Spacing:** 8 px base unit; common steps are 8, 16, 24, 32, 56, and 72
- **Cards:** Graphite fill with a 1 px Rule border; no drop shadows
- **Primary button:** Signal Blue fill, Carbon label, IBM Plex Sans 600 at 15 px
- **Secondary button:** transparent, 1 px Bone border, Bone label
- **Charts:** Signal Blue as the main series, Steel for comparisons, Volt for the one number that matters
- **Imagery:** real screenshots, architecture diagrams, and workspace photos; a stepped motif from the logo can divide sections. Avoid stock robots, glowing brains, and gradient washes.
